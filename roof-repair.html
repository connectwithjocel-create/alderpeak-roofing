Roof repair starts at the source.

Water travels. A stain on the ceiling is rarely directly under the opening. We trace leaks back to where they start, then repair with materials that work with your existing roof.

Request an EstimateRequest an Inspection

01 Overview

Repairs that solve the actual problem

Most roof leaks come from a handful of places: flashing at walls and chimneys, valleys, plumbing vent boots, skylights, and damaged or missing roofing units. The visible symptom — a stain, a drip, a damp spot in the attic — is often several feet away from the cause.

Our repair process focuses on finding the entry point, confirming the extent of damage, and repairing it with compatible materials. If we find the roof is near the end of its service life, we’ll tell you, so you can decide whether a repair or a planned replacement makes more sense.

If water is coming in nowMove valuables, catch drips, and if a ceiling is bulging with water, keep people away from the area. Please don’t go onto the roof — especially in rain or wind.

02 Common problems

What we usually find.

These are the conditions we see most often on this kind of work in Greater Austin.

  1. 01

    Leaks at flashing

    Sidewall, chimney and skylight flashing that has loosened, rusted or lost its sealant.

  2. 02

    Missing or lifted shingles

    Wind-damaged tabs that expose nails and underlayment to rain.

  3. 03

    Cracked pipe boots

    Plumbing vent collars that have split in the sun.

  4. 04

    Valley wear

    Concentrated water flow wearing through shingles or exposing fasteners.

  5. 05

    Damaged metal panels

    Loose fasteners, failed sealant or impact damage on metal roofs.

  6. 06

    Fascia and drip-edge rot

    Water running behind gutters and into the roof edge.

03 How we assess it

How we approach it.

Locate

04 Weighing it up

Benefits and limitations.

An honest picture helps you choose with confidence.

Benefits

  • Lower cost and faster than replacement for isolated problems
  • Stops ongoing damage to decking, insulation and drywall
  • Extends the useful life of a roof in otherwise good condition
  • Documented repairs help with future maintenance planning

Limitations & considerations

  • Repairs can’t reverse overall aging of a worn-out roof
  • Exact color matches for older shingles may not be available
  • Recurring leaks in different areas often signal a bigger issue
  • Some interior damage will need separate drywall or paint repair

05 Frequently asked

Roof Repair questions.

Straight answers to the questions property owners ask most. If yours isn’t here, send it to us.

Ask a question
Can you repair just one section of my roof?

Often, yes, if the rest of the roof is in reasonable condition and compatible materials are available. We’ll tell you if the surrounding roof is too worn for a repair to hold.

Why does my roof leak only in heavy rain?

Wind-driven or heavy rain can push water past flashing, under lifted shingles, or over clogged gutters — paths that stay dry in light rain.

Will a repaired area match the rest of the roof?

We source the closest available match, but weathering means new materials can look slightly different for a while, especially on older shingle roofs.

Is a small leak urgent?

Small leaks can wet decking and insulation for months before showing inside. It’s worth having them assessed promptly.
