Gutters & exteriors that move water away.

A roof sheds water; the gutters, downspouts and edge details decide where it goes next. Done right, they protect fascia, siding, landscaping and foundations.

Request an EstimateRequest an Inspection

01 Overview

Where the roof meets the rest of the building

Many problems blamed on roofs start at the edges: undersized or clogged gutters overflowing behind the fascia, downspouts dumping water next to the foundation, missing drip edge, and soffit vents painted shut. These details are inexpensive compared with the damage they prevent.

We evaluate drainage as part of every roof assessment and handle gutter, downspout and edge work alongside roofing projects — or on its own when that’s what the building needs.

Exterior services

  • Seamless gutter and downspout installation or replacement
  • Gutter sizing for roof area and rainfall intensity
  • Downspout extensions and discharge planning
  • Fascia, drip edge and soffit repairs
  • Soffit ventilation improvements

02 Common problems

What we usually find.

These are the conditions we see most often on this kind of work in Greater Austin.

  1. 01

    Overflowing gutters

    Too small, clogged, or pitched the wrong way.

  2. 02

    Water behind gutters

    Missing drip edge lets water run onto fascia and rot it.

  3. 03

    Foundation splash

    Downspouts discharging right at the slab or beam.

  4. 04

    Separated seams

    Leaking joints on sectional gutters.

  5. 05

    Blocked soffit vents

    Paint, insulation or debris choking off attic intake air.

  6. 06

    Rotted fascia

    Long-term moisture at the roof edge.

03 How we assess it

How we approach it.

Measure

Roof drainage areas and existing gutter capacity.

Trace

Follow water from the roof edge to discharge.

Inspect edges

Fascia, drip edge, soffit and attachment points.

Recommend

Right-sized gutters and discharge locations.

04 Weighing it up

Benefits and limitations.

An honest picture helps you choose with confidence.

Benefits

  • Protects fascia, siding, landscaping and foundations
  • Improves roof-edge durability
  • Can be coordinated with a roof replacement
  • Better attic ventilation through clear soffit intake

Limitations & considerations

  • Gutter guards reduce, but don’t eliminate, cleaning
  • Some lots need grading or drainage work beyond gutters
  • Fascia replacement may reveal additional rot
  • Color matching to existing trim can vary

05 Frequently asked

Gutters & Exterior Services questions.

Straight answers to the questions property owners ask most. If yours isn’t here, send it to us.

Ask a question
Do I need gutters in Austin?

Most homes benefit from them. Central Texas downpours can be intense, and expansive clay soils make controlling water near foundations especially important.

Are seamless gutters better?

Seamless gutters have fewer joints, which means fewer places to leak. They’re formed on site to the length of each run.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

At least once or twice a year, more often under trees such as live oaks that drop leaves and pollen in spring.

Can you add gutters when replacing my roof?

Yes — coordinating them with the roof lets us integrate drip edge and flashing properly.
