02 Local conditions

What we see across the region.

Roofs in each community face similar weather but different housing stock, HOA rules and building ages.

  1. 01

    Austin, TX

    Central neighborhoods mix older homes with low-slope additions and newer infill with standing-seam accents — plus a wide range of commercial property.

  2. 02

    Round Rock, TX

    Established subdivisions where many shingle roofs are reaching replacement age, and hail exposure along the I-35 corridor.

  3. 03

    Cedar Park, TX

    Suburban neighborhoods with HOA guidelines that often shape material and color choices.

  4. 04

    Pflugerville, TX

    Growing communities with many newer homes, where storm checks and early maintenance pay off.

  5. 05

    Georgetown, TX

    Historic downtown buildings alongside large newer developments and active-adult communities.

  6. 06

    Leander, TX

    Fast-growing new construction areas where roof details from builders deserve a second look.

  7. 07

    Lakeway, TX

    Hill Country homes with complex rooflines, tile and metal, and exposure to wind off the lake.

  8. 08

    Bee Cave, TX

    Contemporary and Hill Country architecture with mixed materials and steeper slopes.

  9. 09

    Buda, TX

    A mix of older rural homes and new subdivisions south of Austin along I-35.

  10. 10

    Kyle, TX

    Rapidly expanding neighborhoods with many newer shingle roofs and commercial growth.
