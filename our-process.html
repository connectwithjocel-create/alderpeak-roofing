Five stages. No guesswork.

Every Alderpeak project follows the same sequence — inspect, assess, recommend, plan, review — so you always know where things stand and what happens next.

Request an InspectionRequest an Estimate

01 The process / SHEET P-400

01

Inspect

We start with a close look at the roof and attic.

  • Roof covering, edges, ridges, hips and valleys
  • Flashing at walls, chimneys, skylights and penetrations
  • Attic decking, ventilation and moisture indicators
  • Gutters, downspouts and drainage
  • Photos of every finding
02

Assess

We interpret what we found.

  • Separate active problems from normal wear
  • Identify storm-related versus age-related damage
  • Check compatibility with existing materials
  • Prioritize by urgency and risk
03

Recommend

We lay out your options clearly.

  • Repair, partial replacement or full replacement
  • Material options suited to the structure and style
  • What each option includes and excludes
  • A written scope you can review and compare
04

Plan

We prepare before work begins.

  • Permits and HOA approvals where required
  • Material ordering, delivery and staging
  • Protection for landscaping, driveways and interiors
  • Schedule built around weather windows
  • Communication plan for occupants or tenants
05

Review

We finish with you on site.

  • Final inspection of completed work
  • Cleanup and magnetic nail sweep
  • Photos of key completed details
  • Maintenance notes for your roofing system

02 Frequently asked

Process questions.

Straight answers to the questions property owners ask most. If yours isn’t here, send it to us.

Ask a question
How long does the whole process take?

Inspection to recommendation can often happen within days. Planning time depends on permits, HOA approvals, material availability and weather.

Do I have to commit after the inspection?

No. The inspection is there to give you information. You decide what, if anything, happens next.

Who do I talk to during the project?

You’ll have a point of contact for scheduling, questions and updates throughout.
