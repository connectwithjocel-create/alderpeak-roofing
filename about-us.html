A roofing company built around clear plans.

Alderpeak Roofing & Exteriors serves homeowners and property owners across Greater Austin with roofing work that starts with understanding — and ends with a roof built to handle Texas weather.

Our ProcessContact Us

01 Who we are

Built for the elements. Made to last.

Our name comes from two ideas: the alder, a resilient tree that thrives where conditions are tough, and the peak — the line every roof is built around. Together they describe how we approach roofing: steady, careful work designed to hold up.

We focus on residential and commercial roofing in Austin and the surrounding communities, with services that run from inspections and repairs to full roofing systems and exterior drainage. We believe property owners deserve to see what we see — so we document, explain and recommend, in that order.

Whether it’s a single leak or a complete replacement, the goal is the same: a roof you don’t have to think about.

02 What guides the work

Three principles, one roofline.

They’re in our logo, and they’re in how we approach every project.

The Peak

Every roof has a high point and a weak point. We look for both — then design the work around the weak one.

The Line

Clear lines of communication: what we found, what it means, what it costs, and what happens next. In writing.

The Shield

A roof’s job is protection. Every decision — material, flashing, ventilation — is measured against that.
