02 Anatomy of the system

How a metal roofing system is built.

  1. 01

    Roof deck

    Solid sheathing in sound condition, repaired where needed.

  2. 02

    High-temperature underlayment

    Synthetic underlayment rated for the heat that builds beneath metal.

  3. 03

    Clips and panels

    Panels attached with concealed clips that allow thermal movement.

  4. 04

    Trim and flashing

    Custom-formed eave, rake, ridge and wall flashing in matching metal.

03 Weighing it up

Where it excels. Where it doesn’t.

An honest picture helps you choose with confidence.

Benefits

  • Long service life when properly installed and maintained
  • Sheds water and debris efficiently
  • Reflective finishes can reduce heat absorbed by the roof
  • No exposed fasteners in the panel field on standing-seam systems
  • Distinctive modern appearance

Limitations & considerations

  • Higher upfront cost than asphalt shingles
  • Large hail can dent panels — mostly a cosmetic concern, but visible
  • Oil-canning (waviness in flat areas) can occur
  • Requires experienced installers and specialized tools
  • Repairs need matching panel profile and finish

04 Installation considerations

Details that decide performance.

Whatever the material, the details below separate a roof that performs from one that leaks early.

  1. 01

    Thermal movement

    Panels expand and contract daily; clip systems must allow it.

  2. 02

    Dissimilar metals

    Copper, steel and aluminum in contact can corrode — details matter.

  3. 03

    Penetrations

    Pipes and vents through metal need purpose-made boots.

  4. 04

    Hail exposure

    Thicker gauges and textured finishes reduce visible denting.

  5. 05

    HOA guidelines

    Some communities restrict metal or specific colors.

  6. 06

    Noise

    With solid decking and underlayment, rain noise is typically similar to other roofs.

05 Frequently asked

Metal Roofing questions.

Straight answers to the questions property owners ask most. If yours isn’t here, send it to us.

Ask a question
Is a metal roof louder in the rain?

Installed over solid decking with underlayment and an insulated attic, metal roofs are generally not noticeably louder than other roofs.

Does a metal roof attract lightning?

No. Metal doesn’t increase the likelihood of a lightning strike, and it isn’t combustible.

Can metal roofing go over shingles?

Some systems allow it, but we usually recommend removing old roofing so the deck can be inspected and the assembly is built properly.

What about hail?

Metal resists penetration well, but large hail can leave dents. Panel gauge, profile and finish influence how visible dents are.
